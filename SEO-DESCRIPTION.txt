ThreadPilot — open-source Threads engagement automation for creators, makers, and indie brands.

· Automate authentic comment engagement on Threads posts that match your niche keywords and audience interests.
· Hybrid comment generation: built-in template engine with humanization (lowercase style, mild slang, emoji control) or any OpenAI-compatible LLM endpoint — OpenAI, OpenRouter, Ollama, LM Studio.
· Chrome extension (Manifest V3) reads the live Threads feed, extracts post metadata, and posts comments directly from your logged-in session — no Meta API required.
· Human-like safety: randomized per-post delays, word-by-word typing simulation, configurable daily caps, active-hours scheduling with timezone support (EST, PST, London, Sydney, etc.), and automatic deduplication.
· Real-time dashboard: live activity feed, conversion funnel (scanned → candidate → published → skipped), engagement metrics (likes, replies, CTR), period reports (1h / 24h / 7d / 30d / all-time), and performance charts.
· 100% local: SQLite storage, no cloud dependency, secrets stay on your machine.

Ideal for: creators, product makers, indie brands, and growth marketers who want to drive targeted, top-of-funnel traffic from Threads without paid ads.
